🌌 Study: The Solar System
1. What is the Solar System?

The Solar System is a group of celestial bodies that revolve around the Sun due to its strong gravitational force.

It includes:

The Sun

8 planets

Their moons

Asteroids

Comets

Meteoroids

The Solar System formed about 4.6 billion years ago.

2. The Sun

The Sun is a star at the center of the Solar System.

It contains 99.8% of the system’s total mass.

It provides light and heat, making life on Earth possible.

Made mostly of hydrogen and helium.

3. The Eight Planets

The planets are divided into two main groups:

🔹 Inner (Terrestrial) Planets

Rocky planets close to the Sun.

Mercury

Closest planet to the Sun

Very hot during the day, very cold at night

No atmosphere

No moons

Venus

Hottest planet

Thick atmosphere of carbon dioxide

Rotates opposite to most planets

Similar size to Earth

Earth

Only known planet with life

Has water in liquid form

One moon

Atmosphere rich in oxygen

Mars

Known as the “Red Planet”

Has the largest volcano in the Solar System

Scientists study it for possible past life

Two moons: Phobos and Deimos

🔹 Outer (Giant) Planets

Large planets far from the Sun.

Jupiter

Largest planet

Strong gravity

Famous Great Red Spot (giant storm)

More than 90 moons

Saturn

Known for its beautiful rings

Second largest planet

Very low density (could float in water in theory)

Uranus

Rotates on its side

Blue-green color due to methane gas

Extremely cold

Neptune

Farthest planet from the Sun

Strongest winds in the Solar System

Dark blue color

4. Other Objects in the Solar System
🪨 Asteroids

Mostly found in the asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter.

☄️ Comets

Made of ice and dust

Develop a tail when near the Sun

🌠 Meteoroids

Small rocky particles

When entering Earth’s atmosphere → meteors

If they hit the ground → meteorites

5. Importance of the Solar System

Helps us understand space and Earth’s origin

Allows prediction of eclipses

Supports satellite communication

Encourages space exploration and science
